# My Projects

## Stacked Deck

Klondike solitaire (draw one) that only deals winnable games. A solver plays out every shuffle first,
replays its winning line to confirm it, and throws away the shuffles it cannot win.

- Play: https://csurveykay-byte.github.io/My-Projects/stacked-deck/
- Code: [`stacked-deck/index.html`](stacked-deck/index.html) is the whole game; [`stacked-deck/solver.js`](stacked-deck/solver.js) is the solver on its own.

## Excel & Sheets Shortcuts

A searchable cheat sheet of Windows keyboard shortcuts for Excel and Google Sheets, side by side, with category filters and a light purple theme.

- View: https://csurveykay-byte.github.io/My-Projects/excel-sheets-shortcuts/
- Code: [`excel-sheets-shortcuts/index.html`](excel-sheets-shortcuts/index.html) is the whole page, with no dependencies.
